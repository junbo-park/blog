Collaborative pumpkin carving

19 Oct, 2026

We’ve move to New Jersey suburb about a year and half ago. We don’t do much halloween decoration, but this year we decided to buy some mini pumpkins and place them on our front porch. We thought they looked very cute.

A few days later, we found a couple of our pumpkins were vandalized by our local squirrel population. The pumpkins were strewn about, suggesting the work of a bored teenager, but it seemed unlikely that a teenager would have bitten into the pumpkin, much less repeatedly over multiple days.

At first, I picked up the pumpkins and gingerly placed them again on our porch, thinking that these errant squirrels have mistaken them for something edible and delicious. A week later, now we are missing half of the pumpkins. They weren’t satisfied with taking a few bites and took them home, wherever that may be.

We still have a mini orange pumpkin though, bitten on two sides, showing the seeds inside. I’d like to think that it’s our collaborative decoration project that we have with our local furry artists. The grotesque look indeed fits the season.
