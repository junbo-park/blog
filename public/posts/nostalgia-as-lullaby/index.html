Nostalgia as lullaby

29 Aug, 2026

We have a ten-month old baby at home, and each night we hold her and sing lullabies or hum to her until she falls asleep in our arms. Our favorite is “Island Child” (Korean: 섬집아기). It’s about a baby that falls asleep listening to the sound of the wave, while her mother is out tonging for oysters in the bay. But since the baby can’t tell if a song is an actual lullaby or not, sometimes I just sing to her songs that I like, even if it’s not a lullaby. She still falls asleep because it’s really our voice that calms her, not the song.

Lately, my favorite has been one that I liked since I was a little kid. It’s an odd song for a little kid to fancy since it’s named “Nostalgia” (Korean: 향수). The song is based on a poem that was written in 1920’s by a Korean poet who was reminiscing about his home while he was studying abroad in Japan. The lyrics are poetic, but what I really liked about the song were verses that were sung by an operatic singer. Although I’ve never listened to an opera as a child, I still liked how it sounded.

When I listened to the song in the past, I didn’t pay much attention to the lyric. Now as I tried to sing it from memory, I began to appreciate the lyric, the poem, more. That’s when I decided to translate the poem, as a tribute to this beautiful old poem.

Here’s the original in Korean and the English translation of the poem. I hope I did it justice.


향수
Nostalgia

넓은 벌 동쪽 끝으로
옛이야기 지줄대는 실개천이 회돌아 나가고,
얼룩백이 황소가
해설피 금빛 게으른 울음을 우는 곳,
—그 곳이 참하 꿈엔들 잊힐리야.

Towards the eastern edge of the field
A stream meanders, whispering old stories,
While a spotted bull
Lazily bellows a golden sunset,
How can I ever forget, even in my dreams.

질화로에 재가 식어지면
뷔인 밭에 밤바람 소리 말을 달리고,
엷은 조름에 겨운 늙으신 아버지가
짚벼개를 돋아 고이시는 곳,
—그 곳이 참하 꿈엔들 잊힐리야.

When ashes turn cold in the earthen furnace,
The sound of night wind gallops over the empty field.
The place where my aging dad with shallow wrinkles
Leaned on hay-pillows to fall asleep,
How can I ever forget, even in my dreams.

흙에서 자란 내 마음
파아란 하늘 빛이 그립어
함부로 쏜 활살을 찾으려
풀섶 이슬에 함추름 휘적시든 곳,
—그 곳이 참하 꿈엔들 잊힐리야.

Growing up in dusty country-side,
I missed the azure tint of the sky.
To find a carelessly shot arrow,
I waded through dew-kissed grass.
How can I ever forget, even in my dreams.

전설바다에 춤추는 밤물결 같은
검은 귀밑머리 날리는 어린 누의와
아무러치도 않고 여쁠것도 없는
사철 발벗은 안해가
따가운 햇살을 등에지고 이삭 줏던 곳,
—그 곳이 참하 꿈엔들 잊힐리야.

Where my young sister with jet-black hair,
Flowing like the undulating night waves of a mythic sea,
Where my plain, homely wife,
Barefoot all four seasons on the fields,
Where they reaped harvest, back searing under the sun,
How can I ever forget, even in my dreams.

하늘에는 석근 별
알수도 없는 모래성으로 발을 옮기고,
서리 까마귀 우지짖고 지나가는 초라한 집웅,
흐릿한 불빛에 돌아 앉어 도란 도란거리는 곳,
—그 곳이 참하 꿈엔들 잊힐리야.

In the night sky, stars are rare
Leading us to forgotten sand castles,
Fall crows caw over a roof falling apart,
Where we whispered over the dim lamp light,
How can I ever forget, even in my dreams.
